## 1. Overview of the Three-Volume Trilogy (2025)

The trilogy follows a progression from theoretical exploration to a proposed applied heuristic architecture and then to a dedicated source-reference volume for its mathematical formulation.

### Volume 1 — Theoretical exploratory foundation

**Title:** *Metaphysical Dialogue with AI: Ethnographic Experiment in Digital Ontology - Theoretical Fundamental Architecture (FTA) for Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)*

Published on **21 November 2025**, the first volume is an **842-page university research memoir**, described as a theoretical exploration blending digital ethnography, ontology, and theoretical AI architecture (**FTA–AGI**).

### Volume 2 — Applied theoretical architecture

**Title (bibliographic form):** *Dorian Codex Protocol for Artificial Intelligence - Hamiltonian Theoretical Fundamental Architecture (FTA)*

**Alternative short form used in the description:** *Dorian Codex Protocol for AI - Hamiltonian Theoretical Fundamental Architecture (FTA)*

Published on **17 December 2025**, the second volume is the theoretical fundamental architecture's applied counterpart, describing a proposed **transition from theory to practice**.

For this project, Stefano Dorian Franco presents himself as the inventor of an **applied heuristic mathematical chimera formula**, leveraging and repurposing the architecture of physics:

**Foundational heuristic:** `H_SAFE = T + V - Z`

**Time-dependent formulation:**

```text
H_SAFE(t) = T(t) + V(t) - Z(t)
```

**Correction / iterative expression supplied by the author:**

```text
E(t+1) = E(t) + alpha * nabla(H_SAFE)
```

These expressions are described as a disruptive **Hamiltonian-inspired heuristic mathematical model** intended for the cognitive stabilization of 21st-century artificial intelligence, inspired by the works of **Lagrange and Hamilton**. The analogy is conceptual and methodological; the work does **not** claim to constitute an experimentally validated law of physics or a standard Hamiltonian system.

**Dorian Codex Clockwork from Dorian Codex Protocol for AI** presents and shares **Python/JAX implementation code** of the proposed protocol, intended to allow researchers and coders worldwide to explore and further develop it.

The treatise is described as published in open access under the **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)** license for international academic research. This is an open-access Creative Commons license with noncommercial and share-alike conditions; it should not be confused with an unrestricted software open-source license. License status of each code component should be checked separately.

The research is explicitly situated **outside traditional experimental science**, within a transdisciplinary approach at the intersection of philosophy, AI epistemology, cognitive analysis applied to Large Language Models (**LLMs**), emerging computational ontology, and ontosemantics applied to digital systems.

### Volume 3 — Extended source-reference appendix

**Title (bibliographic form):** *Official Source-reference for DORIAN CODEX H_SAFE - H_safe(t) = T(t) + V(t) − Z(t) - Epistemological Discovery of a Heuristic Mathematical Chimera Equation*

**Longer title wording in the descriptive text:** *Official Source-reference for DORIAN CODEX H_SAFE - H_safe(t) = T(t) + V(t) − Z(t) - Epistemological Discovery of a Heuristic Mathematical Chimera Equation for Artificial Intelligence AGI*

Published on **31 December 2025**, the third volume is an extended appendix and source-reference for the **H_SAFE** formulation and its conceptual objectives.

The author's description of the framework is:

> The Dorian Codex H_safe (H_SAFE(t) = T(t) + V(t) - Z(t)) is a heuristic mathematical formula created as the engine for the AI theory equation “Dorian Codex Protocol for Artificial Intelligence - Hamiltonian Theoretical Fundamental Architecture (FTA)”. Established in 2025 in Turin and Paris by the Italo-French author Stefano Dorian Franco, it operates as a homeostatic monitoring layer utilizing standard mathematical and physics tools—derived from the 19th-century works of Lagrange and Hamilton—repurposed during the 2020 pre-AGI decade toward the finality of internal cognitive stability and safety for AI evolution into AGI new paradigms. This heuristic is explicitly, in-design, a “mathematic chimera” engineered to unveil a 5D multidimensional Pentacle map on the data-hidden part of the cognitive ontosemantic universe map of AGI evolution.

This passage is reproduced as an authorial description of the proposed heuristic, not as a claim of independently demonstrated AGI capability.

### Intellectual and methodological continuity

Franco’s multidisciplinary path is presented as a continuous cultural exploration of **meaning, consciousness, and the changing relationship between humans**, drawing both on ancestral rituals and on emerging intelligent digital systems. His approach is independent and experimental, based on the view that creativity expands when no discipline, genre, or medium is treated as a boundary.
